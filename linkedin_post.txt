🌳 Task 3 Complete — Decision Tree Classifier for Bank Marketing Prediction | SCT_DS_3

Excited to share the completion of my third task at SkillCraft Technology as a Data Science Intern!

🎯 Objective: Build a Decision Tree Classifier to predict whether a bank client will subscribe to a term deposit based on their demographic and behavioral data.

📊 Dataset: UCI Bank Marketing Dataset — 4,521 real-world records from direct marketing campaigns of a Portuguese banking institution.

🔧 What I Did:
• Preprocessed the dataset using one-hot encoding for all categorical variables
• Split the data into 70/30 train/test sets for robust evaluation
• Trained a DecisionTreeClassifier (max_depth=3) for an interpretable, production-ready model
• Evaluated performance with accuracy scoring and a full classification report
• Visualized the decision tree to understand the key factors driving predictions

💡 Key Takeaway: Decision trees offer incredible interpretability — you can trace exactly how the model makes each prediction. Features like call duration, previous campaign outcome, and contact month emerged as the most influential factors in predicting customer behavior.

🛠️ Tech Stack: Python | pandas | scikit-learn | matplotlib

This project reinforced my understanding of the full ML pipeline — from data preprocessing and feature encoding to model training, evaluation, and visualization.

Grateful to SkillCraft Technology for this hands-on learning opportunity!
